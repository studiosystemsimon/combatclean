Combat Clean — Generator drop weights

What each generator drops when you tap it. A generator has its OWN level (0–4, raised by merging generators); at level L a tap rolls a tile TIER from that level's weighted table. Weights are relative — the roll normalises by the row total (rollDropLevel), so the % below = weight ÷ row-total. Each tap costs 1 energy.

SNAPSHOT · src/data/config/game/generators/*.json
How to read it. Row = the generator's current level. Cells = chance of dropping each tile tier (T0 = base, up to T4). The gold-outlined cell is the most likely drop at that level. Higher generator level → higher-tier tiles (the whole distribution shifts right as you merge generators up).
Blade vs Range/Magic. Range and Magic share identical tables; Blade is a touch stingier on the tier-up at low levels (e.g. Lv1: 20/35% vs 20/34% for T1, Lv4 total 70 vs 72).
Source of truth. These are baked from src/data/config/game/generators/<key>.json → dropsByLevel (read at runtime via content.ts → C.GENERATORS). Edit the JSON to retune; this page is a snapshot.

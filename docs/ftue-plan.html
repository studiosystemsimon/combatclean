Combat Clean — FTUE concept map & teaching order

Every teachable system, and the order to introduce them, weighted by excitement · criticality · ease · understanding-unlock.

§How the order was chosen

The sequence balances four forces. Teach early when a concept scores high on all four; teach late when it's complex, optional, or only makes sense after earlier concepts. A concept is never introduced before the thing it depends on.

🎇 Excitement
Does it hook / feel great in the first seconds? Lead with spectacle and payoff, not rules.
🎯 Criticality
Must the player understand it to play the core loop at all? Non-negotiables come first.
🧩 Ease
How fast is it grasped from one tap? Easy wins build momentum before harder ideas.
🔓 Unlocks understanding
Does learning it make the rest of the game click? Teach the "keystone" ideas early.

§Recommended FTUE sequence

Five phases. Phases A–B are the mandatory guided opening (the core loop); C onward can be lighter coachmarks or discovered naturally. active coachmark = stop & guide · passive nudge = highlight, no gate · defer = later session / on first encounter.

Phase A · The Hookfirst 0–20s
1
Auto-battle: your heroes fight for you Combat
Open ON a live fight already in progress — hits landing, HP bars moving, a wave clearing. Say almost nothing.
Why first: highest excitement + it IS the goal of everything else. Zero input required, so it can't be failed. Frames the whole game: "you get stronger, they fight harder."

Phase B · The Core Loop20s–2.5min · guided
2
Tap a generator → a tile drops Board
Point a finger at the blade generator. One tap. A tile pops onto the board.
Why here: the single primary input, dead-easy, instantly satisfying. The player's first act of agency.
🎇 3🎯 5🧩 5🔓 4active coachmark
3
Merge two matching tiles → next tier Board
Drag one tile onto its twin. Big merge pop. "Two of a kind become one, better."
Why here: the title verb — must be taught, learned in one drag, and the pop is a mini-dopamine hit. Everything downstream is "merge to get X."
🎇 4🎯 5🧩 5🔓 5active coachmark
4
Fulfil an order → charge your LIMIT → unleash it Orders + Combat
A Limit Energy Chest order lights gold (you have its tiles). Deliver it → the hero's limit bar surges to full → tap the glowing hero → the limit break wipes the whole wave.
The keystone. This is the ACTUAL loop, not a vague "power up": merges + orders feed limit energy, and a full limit break is what clears the screen. A normal order's limit drip (~10% of a charge) is too small to teach this in one beat, so the FTUE uses a new Limit Energy Chest reward that fills the bar on the spot. Limit break is taught HERE — it's the payoff, not a later extra. (Normal orders also drop gear — taught later, when it matters.)

5
Energy: generating costs energy, it refills over time Board
Teach it exactly when they tap into an empty energy bar — not before. "It fills back up — or play while it does."
Why here: only becomes relevant after they've been tapping. Critical to pacing but boring to hear early; introduce it as the answer to a problem they just hit.

The rarity strip on the pull result / banner: common → primal, colour-coded, with pull odds.

In the hero menu: LEVEL UP (spend hero XP) and EQUIP BEST / LEVEL GEAR (spend gear XP). Lead with the one-tap "auto" buttons.
Why here: the steady power drip that consumes the XP/gear the loop produces. Several sub-actions → medium ease; the auto buttons keep it approachable. Depends on having heroes + resources.

§Full concept inventory (by system)

The complete surface a player eventually meets — including small mechanics folded into the beats above.

Board / Merge

  • Generators — tap to spawn tiles (blade & range at start; magic unlocks later)
  • Merging — 2 same chain + tier → next tier
  • Chains — blade / range / magic ladders
  • Energy — per-tap cost, regenerates over time
  • Cobwebbed tiles — locked; freed by merging a match onto them
  • Board space — finite cells; manage clutter
  • Generator merging — generators themselves level by merging

Orders

  • Orders rail — item requests, completable ones sort left
  • Fulfil — hand over matching tiles → gear chest + power + limit charge
  • Reroll — once per order; keeps rarity / special-ness
  • Special orders — drop an S-tile instead of a chest
  • S-tiles → minigame — merge two S-tiles to trigger it

Combat

  • Auto-battle — heroes attack on their own
  • Waves / clearing — beat the wave to advance the level
  • Focus target — tap an enemy to concentrate fire
  • Limit break — charged ultimate, tap to fire
  • Boss levels / gate — zone-end bosses, manual "challenge" gate
  • Losing / recovery — a loss drops you a level with a shield

Heroes & Roster

  • Squad — your active team + its total power
  • Stats / power — ATK / HP / DEF → one power number
  • Hero leveling — spend hero XP
  • Gear — equip / level / equip-best (gear XP)
  • Ascension — consume a duplicate to rank up
  • Rarities — common → primal ladder + odds

Meta / Progression

  • Map & zones — the level road
  • Area complete — zone clear → generator unlock cinematic
  • Replay + warp-back — farm an old zone, return to frontier
  • AFK income — passive, scales with highest cleared level
  • Gacha / summon — coins → heroes; banners & pity
  • Crystals — ascension material, farmed per zone
  • Currencies — coins · hero XP · gear XP

§Principles baked into this order

• Excitement bookends every phase — spectacle first (auto-battle), the big payoff at the keystone (order → charge limit → limit-break wipe), then gacha and area-clear.
• Teach the keystone loop before anything else — steps 2–4 (tap → merge → fulfil) are the whole game in miniature; the rest is enrichment.
• Teach on demand, not up front — energy, heroes/gear, cobwebs, crystals each appear the moment the player hits the problem they solve.
• Let cinematics & affordances self-teach — area-complete, ascension's pulsing diamond, the gacha reveal, and the minigame need pointing-at, not lecturing.
• Never gate on optional depth — limit break, replay, special orders are discoveries, so a fast player is never slowed and a curious one is rewarded.

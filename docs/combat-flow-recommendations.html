2 · Why it feels bad

Losing punishes twice, silently

Defeat drops you a level and flips on a hidden farm loop. You didn't ask to retreat, and now winning shows no progress — the game quietly replays a lower level until you notice a small button.

One overloaded, part-time button

The same button means "start boss" and "skip forward," and it only appears in recovering/gate states. Its meaning shifts under the player.

Two doors into a boss

Reaching a boss naturally shows a "⚠ BOSS AHEAD" danger gate; jumping forward with the button drops you straight into the boss with no gate. Same event, two experiences.

No "retry" and no "play boss on demand"

You can't simply retry the level you just lost, and a boss is only ever reachable as a side effect of level % 10 == 0.

3 · Recommended flow opinionated default

Three principles: winning always feels like forward motion; losing is a soft setback with one obvious next tap; bosses are a single, deliberate, clearly-labelled gate. One primary button per state, always labelled with its action and its target level.

4 · Decisions for you to make

The recommendation above bakes in some choices. These are the ones worth your explicit call — the recommended pick is marked, but say the word and I'll set any of them differently.

A · On a loss, where does the player land?
▸ Retry the SAME level (recommended) — heroes full-healed, no demotion.
▸ Keep today's behaviour: auto-drop to level − 1.
▸ No penalty at all — just restart, optionally after an ad / small cost.
B · How does a boss start?
▸ Explicit "⚔ Fight Boss" tap at the gate (recommended) — deliberate, dramatic.
▸ Auto-start after a short danger countdown (fully idle-friendly).
C · After a normal win?
▸ Auto-advance (recommended) — momentum, matches an auto-battler.
▸ Require a "Next" tap each level (more control, more friction).
D · Can players farm cleared levels?
▸ Yes, only via the map, with a persistent Resume (recommended) — intentional, never sticky.
▸ No — always snap to the frontier.

5 · Where it changes in code

For when a direction is chosen — the flow lives in a small, well-bounded set of reducer actions plus the arena button. No new systems; this is a rewrite of existing handlers.

File · symbolChange

reducer.ts · RESOLVE_WINKeep frontier auto-advance + boss→gate. Remove the recovering "replay same level" branch (replaced by map-replay + Resume).

My one-line recommendation: take A▸Retry, B▸Explicit gate, C▸Auto-advance, D▸Map-only replay. That removes the silent demotion, gives every state a single clearly-labelled button, and makes the boss the deliberate moment it should be — the smallest change that fixes all four pain points.
